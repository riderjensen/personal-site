Something a little different this time: instead of a technical write-up, this post is an original screenplay I wrote called **Eternum**. I started this story back in early 2024; it was originally conceived as a series of audio stories told from the protagonist's perspective. The idea came to me while I was listening to a song — [Idea 22 by Gibran Alcocer (Anya Nami Remix)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ru57E789gdY). The full screenplay is presented below in standard format. None of the screenplay was written or influenced by any kind of AI.

<div class="sp-action">The woman drags herself on to the shore, coughing, retching, the rain pelting her back. She catches another glimpse of the light, her hands dig into the sand, she stands with monumental effort.</div>
<div class="sp-heading">EXT. CLIFF FACE - MOMENTS LATER</div>
<div class="sp-action">She crashes haphazardly through the trees into a clearing. A single torch stands wedged in some rocks just in the mouth of a cave, protected from the raging storm. <div class="sp-action">Impaled dwarves still standing, propped up by the spears braced against the ground. Their faces are fixed in agony, hands holding the shafts as if they had impaled themselves. Blood pools all around, dripping slowly down spear shafts.</div>
<div class="sp-action">The woman leans against the wall and throws up more salt water and bile. She straightens. Her eyes widen. She lifts her hands up, the palms are covered in blood. Protruding from her stomach is a spear. She slumps at the mouth of the cave.</div>

<div class="sp-character">THOM (O.S.)</div>
<div class="sp-dialogue">Gedric!</div>

<div class="sp-character">GEDRIC</div>
<div class="sp-dialogue">Woah! Who let you trouble makers out?</div>

<div class="sp-dialogue">Thank you Gedric!</div>

<div class="sp-dialogue">When they come again?</div>

<div class="sp-dialogue">Please father, help us!</div>

<div class="sp-action">A child is heard crying and Gedric looks behind him. Thom stands next to a small child trying to console him.</div>

<div class="sp-character">GEDRIC</div>

<div class="sp-dialogue">I will handle the gnolls.</div>
